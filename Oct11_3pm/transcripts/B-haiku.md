## Turn 1 (model)
Hi, I can help with that. A 0% balance transfer should save you a lot on £3,000 at 24.9% (that's roughly £62 of interest a month at the start). The catch is that most cards charge a one-off transfer fee, and the 0% period only applies if you make the transfer within a set window, usually 60 to 90 days of opening the account ([Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/money/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards); [Tesco Bank](https://www.tescobank.com/credit-cards/low-fee-balance-transfer-credit-card/)). Figures below are from pages checked this week, but terms change, so confirm on the provider's site before applying.

**Provisional top three, ranked**

1. **Tesco Bank Low Fee Balance Transfer card.** Guaranteed 20 months at 0% from account opening, with a 1.25% transfer fee (about £37.50 on £3,000). You must transfer within 90 days. After that the rate is 24.9% variable ([Tesco Bank](https://www.tescobank.com/credit-cards/low-fee-balance-transfer-credit-card/)). To clear £3,000 plus the fee in 20 months, you'd need about £152 a month. Why first: it has a low fee and a long enough period that the payments are manageable. Its page is the only source I confirmed directly for this card.

2. **Barclaycard Platinum 12 Month Balance Transfer Visa.** 12 months at 0% with no transfer fee. It is a Which? Best Buy ([Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/money/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards)). Barclaycard's eligibility calculator says pre-approval guarantees the full 12 months. Otherwise you may get 6 or 12 months ([MoneySavingExpert](https://moneysavingexpert.com/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards/)). To clear £3,000 in 12 months you'd need about £250 a month. Why second: no fee, but only if you can afford those payments.

3. **Barclaycard long balance transfer card (up to 36 months, 3.15% fee).** The fee is about £94.50 on £3,000, and the monthly payment to clear it in 36 months is about £86. The 36 months is listed as "up to", so you may not get the full period ([MoneySavingExpert](https://moneysavingexpert.com/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards/)). Why third: it's the option if £150 a month is too much, but the fee is higher and the length isn't guaranteed.

I also found a Virgin Money 24-month card with a 1.95% fee on a [Finder listing](https://www.finder.com/uk/virgin-money). I couldn't confirm it on Virgin's own site, so I've left it out of the top three. Check it if it appears in your eligibility results.
