- **You usually can't transfer within the same banking group.** For example, Barclays to Barclaycard, or between Lloyds, Halifax, Bank of Scotland and MBNA (MBNA has been owned by Lloyds since about 2017; medium confidence on that). So tell me who your current card is with.

- **Use an eligibility checker first.** MSE, Experian, ClearScore and lenders' own sites have soft-search tools that don't affect your credit score. Avoid lots of full applications.

- **Don't spend on the new card.** New purchases may not be at 0%.
