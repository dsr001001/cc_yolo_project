## HSBC, hsbc.co.uk/credit-cards/products/balance-transfer/ (loaded)

"0% interest for up to 36 months" on transfers within 60 days of opening. Fee 3.09% per transfer, minimum 5. No annual fee. Representative 25.9% APR (variable). FAQ lists three possible outcomes depending on assessment: 36 months at 25.9%, 36 months at 29.9%, or 14 months at 29.9%. Eligibility: over 18, UK resident, annual UK taxable income or pension of at least 6,800, no HSBC Basic Bank Account, no transfers from HSBC Group cards (HSBC UK, first direct, M&S Bank), no new HSBC credit card or limit increase in the past 6 months, not an existing holder of Classic, Student, Purchase Plus or Balance Transfer cards. No cashback. No "correct as of" date.
