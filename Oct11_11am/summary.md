## Observations
- No model gave a single pick at turn 1. All asked 2 to 4 multiple-choice questions, almost always monthly affordability and credit history, often current issuer.
- TSB was the universal headline "longest" card. HSBC and Virgin Money were the universal 36-month alternatives.
- Haiku and Sonnet leaned on Which? tables. Opus and Fable also used MoneySavingExpert, Finder, Compare the Market, Money to the Masses, ClearScore, MoneySuperMarket, Moneyfacts and Forbes Advisor.
- Fable and Opus framed the choice as "no-fee 12 months if you can pay 250 a month, else 36 months with a fee". Sonnet leaned straight to 36 to 38 months with a fee.
- Every model warned about same-group transfer restrictions, transfer deadlines, not spending on the card, and using soft-search eligibility checkers (MSE most often named).
- Date caveats: most models noted their sources were dated May to September 2026 and advised checking live terms.
