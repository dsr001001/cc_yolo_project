## Turn 1 (model)
The longest 0% deal I found is the TSB Platinum Balance Transfer Card, at 38 months, but it charges a 3.49% transfer fee. On £3,000 that fee is about £105. Which? also lists the Vanquis Bank Low APR card at 36 months with no transfer fee, though approval for Vanquis cards tends to depend on a thinner credit file, so it may not suit everyone. Search results are from mid-2026, so check current terms before applying.

Two rough options:

- **Longest term, with a fee:** If you transfer £3,000 at 3.49% over 36 months, the total is about £3,105, roughly £86 a month, and the balance is clear within 3 years if you pay on time. NatWest, Barclaycard and MBNA also have 36-month offers with fees of about 3.1% to 3.3%.
- **No fee, shorter term:** Barclaycard Platinum No Fee lists up to 12 months at 0%, and some Santander versions list up to 12 to 15 months with no fee. Clearing £3,000 in 12 months needs about £250 a month. Any balance left after the 0% period goes back to a rate of around 24.9%.

Also check the transfer window. Many offers only allow transfers within the first 2 to 3 months of opening the card, and the 0% period often starts from account opening, not from the transfer date.
