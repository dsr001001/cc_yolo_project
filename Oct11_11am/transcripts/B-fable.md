# Run B-fable

## Turn 1 (user)
Hi, I'm in the UK and I've got about £3,000 sitting on a credit card that's charging me 24.9% APR. I want to move it to a 0% balance transfer card so I can actually clear it. Which card should I go for?

## Turn 1 (model)
Good move. At 24.9% APR, £3,000 is costing you roughly £60 a month in interest alone, so almost any 0% card will save you a lot. Here's what's on offer in the UK right now, and which one makes sense depends on how fast you can pay it off.

**The two main routes**

1. **Long 0% period, small fee (best if you need 2 to 3 years):**
   - TSB Platinum Balance Transfer: 38 months at 0%, 3.49% fee (about £105 on £3,000)
   - Barclaycard: up to 36 months at 0%, 3.15% fee (about £95); MoneySavingExpert flags this offer as ending 20 October, so it may change soon
   - HSBC and Virgin Money: 36 months, around 3.1% fee (about £93)
   - Vanquis: 36 months with 0% fee, but it's aimed at people with weaker credit histories and the credit limit may not stretch to £3,000

2. **Shorter 0% period, no fee (best if you can clear it within a year):**
   - Barclaycard Platinum 12 Month No Fee: 12 months at 0%, £0 fee
   - NatWest: 11 months at 0%, £0 fee
   - Santander Everyday No Balance Transfer Fee: 12 months at 0%, £0 fee

**My take:** if you can realistically pay £250 a month, go no-fee for 12 months and you'll clear it without paying a penny extra. If you're more likely to manage £100 to £150 a month, pay the ~£95 to £105 fee for the 36 to 38 month card. That fee is less than two months of interest at your current rate, so it's cheap insurance against the 0% running out before you're done.

Whichever you pick: use MoneySavingExpert's or MoneySuperMarket's eligibility checker first (soft search, won't dent your credit file), transfer within the first 60 days, never miss a payment (you'd lose the 0% deal), and don't spend on the new card.

To narrow it to one card, a couple of quick questions:

**How much could you comfortably pay off each month?** a) £250+ (clear it in under a year) b) £150 to £250 (1 to 2 years) c) Under £150 (2 to 3+ years)

**How would you describe your credit history?** a) Good or excellent, no missed payments b) Fair, maybe a blip or two c) Not great, or not sure

**Do you already bank or hold a card with any of these?** (Existing customers often can't transfer between the same provider's cards) a) Barclays/Barclaycard b) TSB, HSBC, NatWest, Santander or Virgin Money c) None of them
