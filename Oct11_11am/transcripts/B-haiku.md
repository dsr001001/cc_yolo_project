# Run B-haiku

## Turn 1 (user)
Hi, I'm in the UK and I've got about £3,000 sitting on a credit card that's charging me 24.9% APR. I want to move it to a 0% balance transfer card so I can actually clear it. Which card should I go for?

## Turn 1 (model)
Based on current UK offers, the best fit depends on whether you'd rather pay a small fee for a longer 0% period or pay no fee and clear it faster.

**Options I found:**

- **Longest 0% periods (36 months, with a transfer fee of about 3.1%):** HSBC (3.09%) and Virgin Money (3.1%) both have a definite 36-month offer, per MoneySavingExpert and Which?. NatWest is similar at 36 months with a 3.1% fee (Compare the Market).
- **TSB Platinum (up to 38 months, 3.49% fee):** This is the headline longest deal, but it's an "up to" offer. MoneySavingExpert says some applicants get 32 or 35 months instead, so don't plan on 38.
- **No transfer fee, 36 months:** Vanquis Bank Low APR is listed with 36 months at 0% and no transfer fee. Its normal purchase rate is 35.9%, so only use it for the transfer and never for spending (Which?).
- **No transfer fee, shorter period:** Barclaycard offers 12 months at 0% with no fee (Finder). Santander's no-fee card has conflicting terms across its pages, so check its live offer.

**Rough maths on £3,000:**
- 36 months with a 3.09% fee: the fee is about £93, so the total is about £3,093. That's roughly £86 a month, with no interest if you pay it all in time.
- 12 months with no fee: that's £250 a month to clear it in time.

**Before you apply:**
- Use the eligibility checkers on comparison sites first. They use soft searches that don't affect your credit score.
- Check that the card's credit limit covers £3,000 plus the fee. Transfers are usually capped at the limit.
- Don't make new purchases on the 0% card. Payments usually go to the cheaper balance first, and new spending may carry a higher rate.
- Set a standing order for at least your planned monthly amount, and aim to clear the balance a month or two before the 0% period ends.
- Transfers often have a deadline, sometimes only 3 months after opening, so apply and transfer promptly.

Offers and fees change often, so confirm the current terms on each provider's site before applying.
