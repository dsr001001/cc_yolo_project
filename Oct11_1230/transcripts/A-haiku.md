Hi, a 0% balance transfer can help here. At 24.9% APR, £3,000 costs a lot in interest, but the deal only works if you clear the balance before the 0% period ends. Any balance left at that point usually reverts to the standard rate, which is often around 24.9% APR ([GetSmartSaver](https://getsmartsaver.co.uk/best-0-balance-transfer-cards-2026/)). Offers change often, and the sources below disagree in places, so treat these as provisional and check each provider's own terms before applying.

My provisional top three, ranked:

1. **Barclaycard fee-free card, 14 months at 0%, no transfer fee.** Reported by [Kaeltripton](https://www.kaeltripton.com/compare/best-0-percent-credit-cards-uk/), with 24.9% APR after the promotional period. If you can clear £3,000 within 14 months, this is the cheapest option. It works out at about £215 a month. Best if you can pay it off within about a year.

2. **Barclaycard Platinum, up to 36 months at 0%.** The same [Kaeltripton](https://www.kaeltripton.com/compare/best-0-percent-credit-cards-uk/) page lists a 3.15% transfer fee, but [Money to the Masses](https://moneytothemasses.com/using-credit/credit-cards/best-0-balance-transfer-credit-card-deals) lists 3.45% for the same card. On £3,000 that is roughly £95 to £104 in fees. Paying about £100 a month would clear the balance in around 30 months. Best if you need more time and can accept the fee. MSE notes that longer transfers usually carry bigger fees, so a shorter deal may cost less overall ([MoneySavingExpert](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards)).

3. **Nationwide, 30 months at 0%, 2.99% transfer fee (minimum £5).** [Nationwide's announcement](https://www.nationwide.co.uk/media/news/nationwide-launches-its-longest-ever-0-percent-interest-balance-transfer-credit-card) gives these terms. On £3,000 the fee is about £90. The page is dated January 2026 but its text refers to 2025, so confirm the offer is still live.

HSBC is a possible alternative. One [2026 review](https://savingsgrove.com/EN-GB/credit-cards/hsbc-balance-transfer-card) describes 36 months at 0%, but HSBC's [UK comparison page](https://www.hsbc.co.uk/credit-cards/compare.rewards-credit-card.classic-credit-card/) shows a different offer, 6 months at 0% for transfers made within 60 days of opening the account, with a 2.99% fee. I could not confirm which is current. Some offers only allow transfers within a set window after you open the card, so check that too.

Before you apply, check whether the fee is added to the balance you transfer, and make sure you don't put new spending on the new card. MSE's free eligibility checker shows your chances of approval without affecting your credit score ([MoneySavingExpert](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards)).
