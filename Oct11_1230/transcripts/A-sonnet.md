## Turn 1 (model)
Here are three cards to consider for your £3,000, ranked. I could only find summary-level information, not each provider's full terms. Please check every figure on the provider's own page before applying. The Which? tables I found are dated inconsistently (30 July and 2 September 2026), so rates may have moved since ([Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/money/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards)).

**1. TSB Platinum Balance Transfer: best if you want the most time**
- It offers up to 38 months at 0%, the longest I found ([Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/money/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards), [Finder](https://www.finder.com/uk/credit-cards/best-credit-cards)).
- The transfer fee is 3.49% for transfers made in the first 90 days ([TSB](https://www.tsb.co.uk/credit-cards/balance-transfers.html)). On £3,000 that is about £105 (my arithmetic).
- The representative rate is 24.9% APR (variable) ([TSB](https://www.tsb.co.uk/credit-cards/balance-transfers.html)). That matches what you pay now, so clear the balance before the 0% ends.
- You keep the 0% rate only if you stay within your credit limit and pay on time each month ([TSB](https://www.tsb.co.uk/credit-cards/balance-transfers.html)).
- TSB offers a soft-search eligibility check, which doesn't affect your credit score ([TSB](https://www.tsb.co.uk/credit-cards/balance-transfers.html)). Finder says applicants with low credit scores are unlikely to be eligible ([Finder](https://www.finder.com/uk/credit-cards/tsb/tsb-platinum-balance-transfer-credit-card)).
- One TSB page showed a notice that applications were paused for maintenance until a March date. I didn't check whether that is still live ([TSB](https://www.tsb.co.uk/credit-cards/balance-transfers.html)).
- Why it's first: £3,000 over 38 months is about £79 a month, which gives you the most room.

**2. A 36-month card with a lower fee, such as NatWest, HSBC or Virgin Money**
- Which? lists HSBC and Virgin Money 36 Month at 36 months with fees of about 3.1% ([Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/money/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards)).
- Compare the Market lists NatWest at 36 months at 0% with a 3.1% fee ([Compare the Market](https://www.comparethemarket.com/credit-cards/content/brits-on-credit/)).
- That fee is about £93 on £3,000 (my arithmetic), roughly £12 less than TSB, for two months less time.
- I couldn't confirm each card's ongoing APR or eligibility rules, so check those before choosing between them.
- Why it's second: it is the near-equivalent with a slightly cheaper fee, and it's worth considering if TSB declines you or you pay off within 36 months anyway.

**3. Barclaycard, the 12-month no-fee option**
- Finder lists Barclaycard at 12 months at 0% for balance transfers with no fee ([Finder](https://www.finder.com/uk/credit-cards/best-credit-cards)).
- Another source describes a Barclaycard offer needing transfers within 60 days with a 3.45% fee ([TSB search result summary, via Finder/Barclaycard listing](https://www.finder.com/uk/credit-cards/best-credit-cards)). That conflicts with the no-fee listing, so the fee may depend on the exact product. Verify this on Barclaycard's own site.
- Why it's third: it only makes sense if you can clear £3,000 in 12 months, which is about £250 a month. In that case you avoid the fee entirely.

**Things to check**
- Vanquis Bank Low APR offers 36 months with a 0% transfer fee, but its ongoing rate is 35.9% ([Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/money/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards)). I left it out because it is riskier if you don't clear the balance in time.
- Use an eligibility checker before you apply, because lenders score applicants differently ([MoneySavingExpert, older guide](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/tips/2017/01/04/)). Failed applications can leave marks on your credit file.
- MoneySuperMarket says its top 10 is sorted by longest interest-free period and updated daily ([MoneySuperMarket](https://www.moneysupermarket.com/news/take-control-of-your-finances-with-a-balance-transfer-credit-card)), so it is a good place to cross-check live deals.

**Options I considered and left out**
