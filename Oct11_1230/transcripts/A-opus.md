## Turn 1 (model)
Hi! I checked current UK comparison pages and the card providers' own pages for you. One warning first: these deals change often, and different pages sometimes disagree. I've flagged where they do, so check each figure on the link before you apply.

**How I ranked them:** for £3,000, the best card is the one with the lowest fee that still gives you enough 0% time to clear the debt. That's also [MoneySavingExpert's core advice](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards/). So the right pick mostly depends on how much you can pay each month. The monthly figures and fee amounts below are my own maths.

**1. HSBC Balance Transfer card: best if you want the most breathing room**
- 0% for up to 36 months on balances transferred within 60 days of opening the account ([HSBC](https://www.hsbc.co.uk/content/hsbc/gb/en_gb/credit-cards/products/balance-transfer)). MSE lists it as a "definite" 36 months ([MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards/)).
- Fee of 3.09% per transfer, minimum £5 ([HSBC](https://www.hsbc.co.uk/content/hsbc/gb/en_gb/credit-cards/products/balance-transfer)). On £3,000 that's about £93.
- Representative APR after the deal: HSBC's own page says 25.9% variable ([HSBC](https://www.hsbc.co.uk/content/hsbc/gb/en_gb/credit-cards/products/balance-transfer)), but [MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards/) and [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/money/credit-cards-and-loans/credit-cards/guides/best-credit-card-deals/best-0-balance-transfer-credit-cards) show 24.9%. Go with HSBC's own figure.
- You can't transfer from cards issued by the HSBC Group ([HSBC](https://www.hsbc.co.uk/content/hsbc/gb/en_gb/credit-cards/products/balance-transfer)). MSE says that includes First Direct and M&S Bank ([MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards/)).
- HSBC's page doesn't mention any cashback ([HSBC](https://www.hsbc.co.uk/content/hsbc/gb/en_gb/credit-cards/products/balance-transfer)).
- Why it's first: it's the longest definite 0% period I found, and its fee is slightly lower than the other 36-month cards ([MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards/)). You'd need about £86 a month (£3,093 over 36 months) to clear it.

**2. Tesco Bank low-fee card: best if you can pay about £150 a month**
- Definite 20 months at 0%, with a 1.25% fee and 24.9% APR afterwards ([MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards/)). The fee on £3,000 is about £37.50, so it costs roughly £55 less than HSBC.
- Clearing it in 20 months takes about £152 a month. I couldn't confirm Tesco's transfer deadline on its own site. MSE says transfers usually have to be made within 60 or 90 days of opening the account ([MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards/)), so check the terms.
- A similar option is Virgin Money's definite 18 months at 0.95% ([MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards/)). The fee would be about £28.50, and you'd need about £168 a month.

**3. Barclaycard no-fee card: best if you can pay about £250 a month**
- Up to 12 months at 0% with no transfer fee and 24.9% APR afterwards. If you're not pre-approved, you could get only 6 months ([MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards/)).
- [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/money/credit-cards-and-loans/credit-cards/guides/best-credit-card-deals/best-0-balance-transfer-credit-cards) also lists it as a 12-month, 0% fee Best Buy.
- It ranks third because 12 months is tight for £3,000. If you only got 6 months, it wouldn't work for you.
- Barclaycard's £20 cashback offer is on its 36-month card with the 3.15% fee, not this one ([MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards/)).

**Things to keep in mind**
- How long a 0% deal you get often depends on your circumstances. People with lower credit scores may be offered shorter periods ([Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/money/credit-cards-and-loans/credit-cards/guides/best-credit-card-deals/best-0-balance-transfer-credit-cards)).
- MSE's eligibility calculator lets you check your chances before you apply ([MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/credit-cards/balance-transfer-credit-cards/)).
- Make the transfer within the deadline, and never miss a minimum payment.
